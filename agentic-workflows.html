STORY 01 • RESEARCH

Competitive Research

A competitive research study exploring emerging AI-native products, interaction patterns, enterprise copilots and agentic workflows. The findings informed the design principles and interaction models presented throughout this case study.

Why I Started

As AI-native enterprise products began emerging, I wanted to understand how these experiences differed from traditional CRM platforms.

Instead of benchmarking features, I focused on identifying recurring UX patterns that could influence future enterprise software.

The outcome was a set of UX principles rather than a feature comparison.

Research Takeaways (7 Emerging UX Principles)

PRINCIPLE 01

Design for clear handoff, Preserve conversation context

Preserve complete interaction history and key metadata when transferring tasks between AI agents and human users.

PRINCIPLE 02

Action-Based Handover

Structure handovers around specific entities (cases or tasks) with automated SLA checks and next-step triggers.

PRINCIPLE 03

Support seamless channel continuity

Maintain thread continuity across voice, chat, email, and self-service without requiring users to repeat details.

PRINCIPLE 04

Treat service as part of the brand

Embed consistent brand tone, governance, and empathetic communication standards directly into automated agent workflows.

PRINCIPLE 05

Conversational Agent Builder Experience

Offer accessible, visual builder tools for operators to easily configure, preview, and test autonomous agent flows.

PRINCIPLE 06

Use real-time assistance, not just automation

Provide live co-pilot suggestions, background checks, and contextual summaries to assist human operators in real time.

PRINCIPLE 07

AI-Generated, Context-Adaptive UI ("The UI Is Computed, Not Static")

Generate dynamic interface layouts and adaptive widgets on the fly based on active user state and real-time intent.

Outcome

Synthesized seven recurring AI UX principles from competitive research and translated them into practical enterprise UX principles.

STORY 02 • INTEGRATION

Applying Generative AI to Enterprise B2B Products

Generative AI presents the greatest value when it elevates enterprise workflows through intelligent Human-AI collaboration. This exploration focused on introducing AI capabilities that support faster decision making while preserving familiar user experiences.

Commercial Decision Making: P&L Scenario Simulation & AI Rationales

Problem

Commercial deal leads had to manually calculate profit-and-loss margins across competing discount scenarios, while black-box AI suggestions caused skepticism without clear reasoning.

Decision

Architected an integrated workspace combining side-by-side scenario simulation with a transparent Rationale Drawer detailing financial impacts, risk factors, and confidence evidence.

P&L Summary Report Workspace

The operator initiates deal evaluation within the main P&L Report workspace, analyzing multiple deal simulations (Proposal, Simulation 1, and Simulation 2) side-by-side to assess baseline margins and cash flow metrics.

Conversational Insights & Parameters Grid

Upon launching the assistant, the operator receives an executive summary detailing profitability, IRR, and NPV rationales (left). If requested, the drawer loads a structured parameters grid showing detailed baseline comparison matrices (right).

Outcome

Allowed deal leads to evaluate 3+ pricing scenarios instantly while establishing human-AI trust through 100% transparent reasoning and 1-click override controls.

Proposal Optimization & Score Composition

Problem

Proposal documents required manual proofing for tone, compliance, and product description alignment across large sales teams.

Decision

Embedded a Proposal Scoring Console with inline AI description enhancement popovers and real-time proposal health feedback.

Outcome

Designed Generative AI capabilities that support commercial decision making while integrating naturally into existing enterprise workflows.

STORY 03 • EXPLORATION

Exploring AI-Assisted UX Workflows

Objective

Beyond designing AI-powered products, I explored how AI could also support the UX design process itself.

The goal was to understand how structured knowledge and specialised AI workflows could help designers work more efficiently while keeping people responsible for critical design decisions.

FINAL REFLECTION

What These Explorations Taught Me

Although these explorations addressed different challenges, they all reinforced one consistent belief: the true power of AI lies in driving meaningful Human-AI collaboration. Whether researching AI-native products, integrating Generative AI into enterprise software or rethinking design workflows, the greatest opportunity lies in empowering users through intelligent, transparent co-creation.
