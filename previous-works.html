PROJECT 01
ONSITE UX LEAD Customer Site

United Utilities

Water Utility • North England

Digital transformation and customer experience redesign for North England's leading water utility company. Led the discovery and research phase at the client site to transform their digital presence, majorly across self-service portals, customer-facing corporate website, and mobile experience.

Problem Space & Identified Pain Points

Navigation & Information Overload

Cluttered and unstructured navigation lacking content hierarchy, making key services hard to locate for users.

Complex Mobile Self-Service

Self-service journeys (billing, meter reading) were inaccessible on mobile, leading to excessive customer call-center dependence.

Water Meter Registration Friction

Low customer awareness around water meters combined with complex setup and registration flows.

Communication Transparency

Perception of un-transparent communication during service interruptions and planned maintenance supply outages.

Research Findings & User Evidence

Heuristic Benchmarking

Heuristic evaluation revealed the company B2C site scored 2.18 out of 4 across 11 UX strategy parameters—performing below regional peer utilities (Thames Water, Anglian Water, OVO Energy).

User Survey Evidence

Surveys revealed 49.83% of users failed to find what they needed (293 responses). Primary motivations were viewing statements (53.58%) and paying bills (32.14%) across 101 responses.

Design Direction & Responsive Exploration

Envisaged a solution that logically divided informational content from transactional flows through a dedicated My Account journey. Introduced a personal notification centre to track service requests, positioned prioritized tasks above the fold, and added location-based incident maps for supply outages.

Final Design Outcome & Before/After

Personal Reflection & Learning

“Looking back, I realize that whilst the strategic foundation was in place, the lack of direct access to every user profile forced us to make many decisions regarding users solely based on stakeholder assumptions. All things considered, if I had to do this all over again, I would insist on conducting more user interviews in addition to design workshops to confirm stakeholder assumptions and validate user needs.”

PROJECT 02
OFFSHORE UX LEAD Working closely with Onsite UX Counterpart

STOXX

Financial Services • Zurich • Onsite / Offshore Delivery

Led the offshore UX team while working closely with the onsite UX counterpart to redesign financial index search, complex data tables and market analytics experiences for Europe's leading index provider.

Problem Space & Identified Pain Points

Outdated Experience & IA

Overall website was outdated with information overload, lacking formal information architecture or task prioritization.

Poor Findability & Self-Service

Finding index data was difficult for financial users; self-service provisioning was complex and hidden.

Distinct Target User Groups

Platform needed to serve both Asset Owners (Decision Makers) and Product Issuers (Researchers/End Users).

Personalization & Accessibility Gap

No localized content, accessibility standards compliance, or rules-engine based personalization available.

Research & Competitive Benchmarking Evidence

Competitor & Heuristic Audit

Audited STOXX platform against global index peers (MSCI, FTSE, S&P Dow Jones) across key functional parameters.

User Research & Target Persona
Target User Persona — Kevin (Product Issuer)
Target User Demographics (Survey)

User surveys and persona research identified primary audience split: Asset Owners (Decision Makers) vs Product Issuers (Researchers).

User Journey Mapping & Information Architecture

End-to-End User Journey
Information Hierarchy Matrix

Design Strategy & Team Leadership

Architected a search-first navigational interface with role-based content modules, multi-channel responsiveness, and social media sharing integration. Led the offshore design team of 3 interaction designers and 3 visual designers, coordinating deliverable quality and WCAG accessibility standards.

Final Design Outcome & Before/After

Personal Reflection & Learning

“The best thing this project taught me was leadership management across client expectations, handling a design team, and deliverables. I learned how to deal with people attached to existing ideas, document design stories continuously as you go, and recognize that simplifying complex financial systems takes patient, iterative effort.”

PROJECT 03
ONSITE UX LEAD & PROJECT MANAGER Customer Site

Blackwoods

Industrial & Safety Products • Sydney, Australia

An intense 3-month onshore engagement in Sydney as Project Manager and Lead UX Consultant for Australia's largest industrial and safety products company. Collaborated with marketing, sales, design, technology, and business stakeholders, while leading an offshore design and development team of 5 members to transform the key ordering user journey under stringent timelines.

Problem Space & Implementation Constraints

Order Disruption & Bounce Rate Objective

Users felt lost during ordering due to complex navigation and cluttered information. Design objective: Reduce bounce rate by 20% through a clearer discovery and ordering experience.

Strict Scope & Backend Lock

Redesign was strictly scoped to the order processing journey (Homepage to Checkout) with zero backend functional changes allowed.

Layered Legacy CSS Constraints

Legacy codebase had heavily layered CSS and inline styles, complicating stylesheet updates without breaking non-scoped pages.

Restricted QA Access

Lack of direct QA environment access required local machine testing prior to QA deployment, placing severe demands on release cycles.

Expert Audit & Legacy Interface Evidence

Heuristic Showstopper Audit

Conducted website audit uncovering critical H7 (Flexibility & Efficiency of Use) violations due to cluttered mega-menus wasting vital screen real-estate.

Outdated Search & Product Listing

Legacy search listing screen suffered from poor filter categorization, hidden pagination, and low visual hierarchy.

Design Strategy & Wireframe Architecture

Positioned popular and most-searched categories upfront on the homepage, made search prominent, and improved product listing screens with better filter categorization, sorting, and pagination options. Design objective: Reduce bounce rates through a learnable, intuitive homepage-to-checkout journey.

Final Visual Design Outcome

Personal Reflection & Learning

“Leading both client-facing UX consulting in Sydney and an offshore delivery team taught me how to operate in a Lean UX process under tight 3-month timelines. Handling client expectations, managing multiple teams, and navigating legacy CSS constraints reinforced that direct user interviews alongside workshops are crucial to validate stakeholder assumptions.”
