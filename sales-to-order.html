Understanding the Sales-to-Order Platform

Enterprise sales operators faced fragmented validation rules, hidden checkout errors, and 5-step catalog navigation loops across B2B CPQ, Commerce, and Order Fulfillment tools. This case study details how we simplified multi-app complexity into a unified, intent-driven commercial experience.

PRODUCT CONTEXT

B2B Telecom Sales CPQ & Enterprise Commerce Suite powering multi-product bundle configuration.

DESIGN SCOPE

Offer Discovery Workspaces, Real-Time Validation Safeguards, and Cross-App Async Notification Frameworks.

DESIGN PERSPECTIVE

"Enterprise software often fails not from a lack of features, but from excessive cognitive friction. Designing for complex B2B systems requires shifting from feature-centric forms to intent-driven workflows. By structuring business logic into clear, non-blocking interaction models, we enable enterprise operators to make confident decisions without getting bogged down by multi-app fragmentation."

STORY 01

Reducing Offer Discovery Complexity

Problem

B2B product catalogs required sales operators to click through 5 nested sub-category pages just to discover available B2B commercial bundles, adding significant delay to proposal generation.

Business Context

In enterprise telecom sales, rapid quote generation directly impacts deal velocity. When sales operators face multi-nested catalog trees, offer selection overhead increases cognitive friction and leads to proposal drop-offs.

Design Strategy

Shifted the offer discovery paradigm from a multi-level hierarchical tree to a flat, intent-driven workspace. By introducing sub-category tabbed navigation alongside instant bundle preview cards and automated serviceability checks, we enabled operators to evaluate and attach commercial bundles within a single contextual view.

STORY 02

Making Enterprise Validation Actionable

Problem

Enterprise validation rules were hidden deep within multi-tab forms. Sales operators received blocking modal errors late during checkout, forcing them to re-verify quotes manually across legacy tools.

Business Context

Commercial proposal creation across telecom CPQ and Commerce tools involves hundreds of complex compatibility and pricing rules. Late-stage blocking modals broke operator flow and required manual cross-system audits.

Design Strategy

Architected a non-blocking validation framework that decouples rule evaluation from user input locks. By introducing real-time header badges, persistent drawer popovers, and auto-scroll-to-field links, operators receive immediate error visibility without being blocked from completing parallel data entry tasks.

STORY 03

Designing Cross-Application Async Notifications

Problem

B2B operations involve long-running background processes (e.g. bulk price recalculations, serviceability checks, order fallout resolution). Operators suffered from silent backend failures and zero visibility into background task completion.

Business Context

Enterprise transactions often process off the main thread. Without transparent background status tracking, operators frequently resubmitted duplicate orders or abandoned stuck provisioning tasks.

Design Strategy

Before designing UI elements, I mapped every asynchronous trigger, feedback state, and refresh behavior across Proposal and HVO workflows to uncover common systemic patterns. Derived from this systematic discovery, I created a cross-application Asynchronous Process Notification Framework that communicates task states (processing, success, partial completion, failure) through unified toast banners and a central Process Monitoring drawer.

DISCOVERY ARTIFACT

Mapping Existing Async Processes

Before designing a reusable notification framework, every asynchronous trigger, feedback state and refresh behaviour across Proposal and HVO workflows was mapped to identify inconsistencies and discover reusable interaction opportunities.

What this mapping uncovered

  • • Multiple asynchronous process triggers existed across Proposal and HVO workflows.
  • • Feedback behaviour was inconsistent across products.
  • • Refresh behaviour varied depending on workflow and process completion.
  • • A reusable notification framework could standardise the experience across applications.

Key Design Insight

"Rather than designing individual notifications for every workflow, I first identified common behavioural patterns across the ecosystem and then designed one reusable notification framework that could scale across multiple enterprise products."

Key Learnings & Design Insight

  • Non-Blocking Background Tasks: Decoupling heavy API execution from active user input allows operators to remain productive.
  • In-Context Diagnostic Tracing: Providing direct error logs inside notification drawers speeds up support handoffs.

Legacy Experience & Root-Cause Analysis + Expand Details

In the legacy catalog workflow, operators navigated 5 nested sub-category pages to find commercial offers. Validation errors occurred late during checkout as blocking modal dialogs, requiring complete order form re-validation and causing severe operator drop-offs.

Serviceability & Location Coverage Framework + Expand Details

Integrated automated serviceability checks directly into offer discovery views. Operators verify network coverage and location availability in real time prior to adding complex commercial bundles to quotes.

Validation Artifacts & Design System Specs + Expand Details

Established standardized component tokens for toast alert banners, floating rule popovers, and status badge counts. Specs were documented in Figma and storybook, enabling engineering teams across 4 product suites to consume reusable validation components out of the box.

Edge Cases, Trade-offs & Lessons Learned + Expand Details

Key trade-off: Prioritized cross-application design system alignment over custom single-app shortcuts. Lesson learned: In complex B2B ecosystems, non-blocking feedback models outperform strict step-by-step wizard forms by preserving operator velocity.
